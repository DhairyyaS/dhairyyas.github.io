[ 01 ]  About

I'm a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Princeton Neuroscience Institute, working with Dr. Ken Norman. I previously completed my Ph.D. in Psychology at the University of Pennsylvania, advised by Dr. Anna Schapiro. Prior to that, I studied Psychology and Computer Science at Ashoka University and worked with Dr. Bittu Rajaraman.

I'm interested in how brains and machines learn and remember - including the range of factors that shape how experiences are encoded and reorganized into lasting, structured knowledge. My work pairs neural-network modeling with behavioral experiments, focusing on how hippocampal and neocortical regions interact to allow us to perform rich and complex memory functions.

I'm happy to chat about anything related to these topics. Feel free to drop me an email.
